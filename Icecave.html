Box in the 1976 Film

In Logan's Run, Box is the eerie machine Logan 5 and Jessica 6 encounter in the frozen caverns while searching for Sanctuary. Roscoe Lee Browne voiced and performed the character.

Built to bring food back

Box was sent outside the domed city to collect seafood and other marine food for its people. Alone for a long time, it developed a theatrical personality and filled its icy cavern with sculptures of birds.

When the food stopped coming

Box proudly lists its stores:

“Fish, plankton, sea greens and protein from the sea. Fresh as harvest day.”

Then it explains that the food supply ended. When human fugitives began arriving through the tunnels, Box treated them as a substitute harvest and froze them for storage. Logan and Jessica fight their way out, and the cavern collapses around Box.

The Novel's Different Box

Box is portrayed very differently in William F. Nolan and George Clayton Johnson's 1967 novel. There, Box is a human criminal exiled to an ice wasteland—not the food-collecting machine from the film.

Why the Ice Cave Matters

  • Logan and Jessica think they may have reached the legendary Sanctuary.
  • Box's frozen collection reveals that the tunnel is another deadly trap.
  • Escaping the cave sends them toward the outside world, a place they have never seen.
